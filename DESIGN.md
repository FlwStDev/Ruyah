# Ruyah

Visual direction taken from the Ferrari analysis in the design collection: cinematic black, white type, and one red used almost nowhere. This is not Ferrari. No prancing horse, no Ferrari name, no livery.

## Atmosphere

The canvas is `#181818`. Type is white at a modest weight. Body copy is `#969696`. Corners are square. Space is wide. Red `#da291c` is allowed on the primary action and on the thin mark for the current page. A white band is allowed for a form that sits against the black, the way a listing sits in a black editorial page.

## Type

Inter, one family. Titles around 48px, weight 500, slight negative tracking. Navigation and buttons are 11–13px, semibold, uppercase, wide tracking.

## Do not

- Do not round the buttons, cards, or navigation.
- Do not fill a navigation row with red.
- Do not add a second accent, a serif, or a warm paper background.
- Do not put a shadow under a card.
